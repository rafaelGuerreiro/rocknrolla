RocknRolla — Rollers · two-layer rig
The body rolls. The face stays put.
Each roller is two separate layers: a faceless body that spins with the physics, and a face pinned upright over its centre. Swap the face symbol to change the expression at any time — happy, determined, dizzy on impact — without touching the body art.
